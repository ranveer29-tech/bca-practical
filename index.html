Alan

Alan Turing was born on June 23, 1912, in Maida Vale, London, to an upper-middle-class family and spent much of his childhood separated from his parents while his father served in the Indian Civil Service.

• Born: June 23, 1912, at Warrington Lodge in Paddington, London. • Parents: Julius Mathison Turing and Ethel Sara Turing (née Stoney). • Family Roots: His father was a British civil servant in the Indian Civil Service, and his mother was the daughter of a railway chief engineer. He had an older brother named John. • Early Upbringing: Because his parents traveled back and forth to India, Alan and his brother spent their early years living with a retired army officer (and later the Ward family) in St Leonards-on-Sea, Sussex.
