what is computer ?

At its core, a computer accepts raw data, processes it through logical and mathematical operations using a binary system (1s and 0s), and outputs the final result. The term once referred to a human performing calculations, but it now universally means automated electronic machinery.

A programmable electronic device designed to accept data, perform prescribed mathematical and logical operations at high speed, and display the results of these operations. Mainframes, desktop and laptop computers, tablets, and smartphones are all types of computers.
